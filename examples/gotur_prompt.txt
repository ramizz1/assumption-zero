## Product overview

**Götür.az** is an Azerbaijani online marketplace available as a web platform and mobile application. It is intended to let individuals and businesses:

* Sell new or used products.
* Buy products from other users and stores.
* Rent products instead of purchasing them.
* Exchange products through barter.
* Publish listings with photos, descriptions, prices, categories, locations, and seller information.
* Search, filter, browse, save, and manage listings.
* Communicate through an integrated chat system.
* Send images and voice messages in chat.
* Create user profiles with avatars and listing history.
* Use AI-assisted functionality to simplify listing creation, improve descriptions, categorise products, and help users interact with the marketplace.
* Potentially publish "wanted" requests describing something they want to buy, rent, or exchange.
* Use the service through both mobile and desktop devices.

## Core value proposition

Götür.az aims to combine buying, selling, renting, barter, communication, and AI assistance in one Azerbaijan-focused platform. Its differentiation should not be described as "having more features" alone. Analyse whether these features solve meaningful user problems and determine which one could create the strongest reason for users to switch from existing platforms.

The platform must serve both sides of the marketplace:
1. Sellers, stores, service providers, and people with unused products.
2. Buyers, renters, and people searching for specific products.

The central marketplace challenge is liquidity: Götür.az needs enough quality listings to attract buyers and enough active buyers to motivate sellers.

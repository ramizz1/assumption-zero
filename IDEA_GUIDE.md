# Step-by-Step Guide: How to Get 100% Accurate MVP Validation Results

Assumption Zero stress-tests startup ideas by gathering evidence from real research providers (Live Web Search, News, Arxiv, GitHub, Hacker News, Wikipedia, Reddit) and evaluating it through 3 distinct AI perspectives (Market Analyst, Skeptical Investor, Practical Builder).

To get the most accurate Opportunity Score, realistic competitor lists, and actionable validation experiments, follow this step-by-step guide when inputting your idea.

---

## 1. Product Name & 1-Sentence Pitch
- **What to do:** Give a clear product name and a 1-sentence summary of what it does.
- **Good:** `ShopStream — Live video shopping platform connecting local boutique merchants with mobile buyers.`
- **Bad:** `E-commerce app.`

---

## 2. Problem Being Solved (Crucial)
- **What to do:** State who suffers, what specific process fails today, and why existing solutions fall short.
- **Good:** `Independent boutique fashion merchants struggle to showcase fit and fabric quality using static photos on Instagram, resulting in 35% return rates and low conversion.`
- **Bad:** `Making it easier to sell clothing.`

---

## 3. Target Customer & Geography
- **What to do:** Be specific about the exact audience and geographical market.
- **Good:**
  - *Customer:* `Independent boutique apparel merchants and Gen-Z fashion shoppers`
  - *Geography:* `United States`
- **Bad:**
  - *Customer:* `Everyone`
  - *Geography:* `Global`

---

## 4. Business Model & Pricing
- **What to do:** Specify how you intend to generate revenue.
- **Good:** `8% transaction fee on live stream sales + $29/mo merchant store subscription.`
- **Bad:** `Make money.`

---

## 5. Founder Advantage & Budget
- **What to do:** State your technical background and available capital/runway.
- **Good:**
  - *Skills:* `Full-stack developer (React + Node.js + WebRTC live streaming)`
  - *Budget:* `$10,000 budget, 6 months runway`
- **Bad:**
  - *Skills:* `None`
  - *Budget:* `$0`

---

## 6. Known Competitors & Alternatives
- **What to do:** List existing competitors or manual alternatives (Excel, Instagram Live).
- **Good:** `Whatnot, Popshop Live, Instagram Live, TikTok Shop`
- **Bad:** `No competitors exist`

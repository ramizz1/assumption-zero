# Step-by-Step Guide: How to Get 100% Accurate MVP Validation Results

Assumption Zero stress-tests startup ideas by gathering evidence from real research providers (GitHub, Hacker News, Wikipedia, Reddit) and evaluating it through 3 distinct AI perspectives (Market Analyst, Skeptical Investor, Practical Builder).

To get the most accurate Opportunity Score, realistic competitor lists, and actionable validation experiments, follow this step-by-step guide when inputting your idea.

---

## 1. Product Name & 1-Sentence Pitch
- **What to do:** Give a clear product name and a 1-sentence summary of what it does.
- **Good:** `Gotur.az — P2P listing & rental marketplace in Azerbaijan connecting buyers & sellers with instant chat and boost packages.`
- **Bad:** `Marketplace in Azerbaijan.`

---

## 2. Problem Being Solved (Crucial)
- **What to do:** State who suffers, what specific process fails today, and why existing solutions fall short.
- **Good:** `Local sellers and buyers in Azerbaijan struggle with high fees and slow listing approvals on existing platforms. Sellers lack instant mobile chat and low-cost boost options.`
- **Bad:** `Making it easier to buy and sell.`

---

## 3. Target Customer & Geography
- **What to do:** Be specific about the exact audience and geographical market.
- **Good:**
  - *Customer:* `Individual peer-to-peer sellers and local SMB merchants`
  - *Geography:* `Azerbaijan`
- **Bad:**
  - *Customer:* `Everyone`
  - *Geography:* `Global`

---

## 4. Business Model & Pricing
- **What to do:** Specify how you intend to generate revenue.
- **Good:** `Freemium listing marketplace with $2–$10 boost promotion packages & premium store subscriptions.`
- **Bad:** `Make money.`

---

## 5. Founder Advantage & Budget
- **What to do:** State your technical background and available capital/runway.
- **Good:**
  - *Skills:* `Full-stack developer (Nuxt.js + Django REST + Flutter)`
  - *Budget:* `$5,000 budget, 6 months runway`
- **Bad:**
  - *Skills:* `None`
  - *Budget:* `$0`

---

## 6. Known Competitors & Alternatives
- **What to do:** List existing competitors or manual alternatives (Excel, WhatsApp groups).
- **Good:** `tap.az, lalafo.az, Facebook Marketplace groups`
- **Bad:** `No competitors exist`

---
